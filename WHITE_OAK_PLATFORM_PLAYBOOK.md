# White Oak Platform Playbook

**Status:** Living business, design and delivery document  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Case study:** 7845 Torrison Drive / White Oak  

## 1. Product definition

White Oak is both:

1. A tailored concept presentation for the current property owner.
2. The first demonstrable case study for a premium digital-property service.

The service is not sold as a generic website template. It is sold as a process for directing a memorable digital experience around a real place.

**Category statement:** Interactive property documentary and luxury showcase platform.

## 2. How this becomes a sales asset

A future prospect should be shown the work in this order:

1. The original property and business challenge.
2. The three White Oak concepts.
3. The most important UX choices inside each concept.
4. The customer’s selected design recipe.
5. The final production website.
6. A short explanation of which systems can be reconfigured for the prospect’s property.

The pitch is not “choose one of these templates.” It is:

> We explored three premium ways to tell the White Oak story, helped the customer identify what felt right, and turned those decisions into a cohesive final experience. We can run the same disciplined process for your property.

## 4. The three demonstration concepts

### Editorial Residence

Use when the property’s strengths are architecture, design, provenance, privacy and prestige.

Demonstrates:

- Asymmetric editorial composition
- Overlapping photography
- Fine typographic hierarchy
- Restrained invitations rather than aggressive booking widgets
- Magazine-style spatial storytelling

### Luminous Cinema

Use when the property’s strengths are atmosphere, landscape, day-to-night transformation and emotional memory.

Demonstrates:

- Full-screen media
- Four-act narrative direction
- Scroll-controlled camera drift
- Opposing image and copy movement
- Soft dissolves and signature moments
- Muted, optional concept ambience
- Emotionally named chapters

### Location Dossier

Use when the property must qualify production, event or operationally focused leads.

Demonstrates:

- Immediate audience routing
- Structured property facts
- Annotated visual range
- Logistics, program and FAQ modules
- Explicit scout, proposal and availability actions

## 5. Shared service architecture

Every engagement should separate four layers:

### Property truth

Verified facts, approved photography, real availability destinations, policies, capacity, services and contact details.

### Story direction

The emotional premise, audience priority, narrative sequence, chapter language and final memory.

### Experience modules

Heroes, galleries, chapter transitions, facts, room navigation, case studies, inquiries and booking handoffs.

### Visual expression

Typography, spacing, cropping, palette, motion intensity, surface treatment and responsive composition.

The experience modules may be reused. Property truth, story direction and visual expression must be tailored.

## 6. Customer decision recipe

The final direction is documented as a recipe rather than “Concept 1 won.”

Record:

- Base concept
- Hero model
- Navigation model
- Typography and palette
- Motion level
- Story sequence
- Gallery model
- Property-information density
- Sound decision
- Stay / event / production priority
- CTA tone
- Contact and booking flow
- Mobile simplifications
- Features borrowed from the other concepts

This prevents a hybrid from becoming an incoherent collection of favorite pieces.

## 7. Authenticity rules

- Never replace real property photography with generated property evidence.
- Never invent clients, publications, testimonials, renovation history or capacity.
- Concept media is visibly identified during review.
- Ambient sound remains muted by default.
- Prototype ambience must be replaced with authentic recordings before production.
- Before/after interactions require matched, truthful source images.
- Floor-plan interactions require an approved plan or an explicit conceptual label.

## 8. Motion philosophy

> Every scroll should feel like the next shot in a beautifully directed film. Every transition should have a purpose. Every pause should allow the visitor to appreciate the space before continuing the journey.

Motion must explain space, guide attention, create continuity or control pacing. If it cannot answer which of those jobs it performs, remove it.

Use two to four signature moments per long page. Allow calm editorial sections between them.

## 10. Case-study completion checklist

When White Oak is finalized, add:

- Approved final direction
- Screenshots of the three reviewed concepts
- Customer-selected feature recipe
- Final desktop and mobile presentation
- Before/concept/final comparison
- Verified outcomes or customer feedback
- Links to the concept presentation and final website
- A clear list of reusable systems demonstrated

Until approval, label the current site **Concept Presentation**, not final customer work.
